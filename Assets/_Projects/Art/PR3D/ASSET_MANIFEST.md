## Import notes

- Unity-ready FBX variants are retained unchanged under each pack's `original/Models` folder.
- Original atlas textures are retained unchanged under each pack's `original/Textures` folder.
- Preview materials and prefabs belong under `Assets/_Projects/Art/PR3D/KitchenBackground/`.
- Decorative background objects must not add colliders or change Level 301 gameplay data.
- The preview targets portrait camera framing; important decoration stays outside the central interaction silhouette.
